There's something magical about fall in Cache Valley. The leaves along the Logan River turn gold and crimson, the air gets that unmistakable crispness, and the mountains put on a show that never gets old. But as the season shifts, your home needs a little attention too. Before you break out the cozy blankets and hot cocoa, it's worth giving your space a solid refresh so you can enjoy every cozy minute of autumn without worrying about dust, grime, or summer's leftover mess.

Here's our family-friendly fall home cleaning checklist — put together by the Hero Cleaners team right here in Logan, UT — to help you get your home ready for the best season of the year.

## Deep Clean Your Windows Before the Frost Hits

Summer storms, sprinkler spray, and months of pollen leave your windows looking hazy. Fall is the perfect time for a thorough window cleaning, inside and out, before freezing temperatures make the job a whole lot harder. Clean windows let in more of that gorgeous golden autumn light, which makes a bigger difference than you'd think.

Don't forget window tracks and sills — they collect dust and dead bugs all summer long. A quick vacuum and wipe-down can make your windows look brand new. And if you'd rather not climb a ladder yourself, Hero Cleaners offers professional window washing throughout Cache Valley so you can skip the hassle entirely.

## Tackle the Kitchen Top to Bottom

Your kitchen works overtime during summer — barbecues, family gatherings, kids grabbing snacks all day. By fall, your oven, fridge, and pantry could probably use some extra love.

Here's a quick kitchen punch list:

- **Oven and stovetop:** Degrease and scrub the burners, oven interior, and range hood.
- **Refrigerator:** Pull everything out, toss expired items, and wipe down shelves and drawers.
- **Pantry:** Organize dry goods, check expiration dates, and wipe shelves clean.
- **Sink and disposal:** Scrub the basin and freshen your garbage disposal with baking soda and lemon.
- **Small appliances:** Clean the toaster, microwave, and coffee maker — crumbs and splatters add up fast.

A clean kitchen makes holiday baking and fall cooking so much more enjoyable. You deserve a fresh start heading into the season.

## Refresh Your Bathrooms for the Cozy Season

When the weather turns cold in Logan, UT, you spend more time indoors — which means your bathrooms get more traffic. Now's the time to give them a deep clean.

Scrub grout lines, descale showerheads, and clean behind toilets where dust loves to hide. Swap out old shower curtains or liners, wash bath mats, and restock with fresh towels. If you have bathroom exhaust fans, pop off the covers and vacuum the dust — they'll run more efficiently and keep moisture under control as you start taking those long, warm showers.

## Dust Every Forgotten Corner

Summer's open windows bring in fresh air, but they also invite in dust, allergens, and tiny particles that settle everywhere. Fall is a great time to go beyond the basics:

- Dust ceiling fan blades (you'll be switching them to clockwise rotation soon).
- Wipe down baseboards and door frames.
- Clean light fixtures and lampshades.
- Vacuum under furniture and behind couches.
- Dust blinds, shutters, and curtain rods.

These forgotten spots make a huge difference in your home's air quality — especially important for Cache Valley families dealing with fall allergies.

## Prep Your Floors for Mud Season

Anyone who's lived through a Logan, UT autumn knows what happens when rain, fallen leaves, and muddy boots meet your entryway. Getting your floors in great shape now will help them survive the months ahead.

Vacuum and deep-clean carpets to pull out summer dirt. Mop hard floors with a quality cleaner. Place durable mats at every entry point and consider a shoe rack or boot tray near the front door. If you have area rugs, now is the time to shake them out or have them professionally cleaned.

## Don't Forget the Laundry and Linens

Pull out your fall and winter bedding and give it a good wash before it goes on the beds. Dust settles on stored linens over the summer, so freshening them up before use makes a real difference. While you're at it, flip or rotate your mattresses and vacuum them to remove dust mites.

Wash couch cushion covers, throw blankets, and any decorative pillows that have been out all summer. Starting the season with clean, fresh-smelling linens is one of life's simple pleasures.
