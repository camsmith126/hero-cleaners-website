## What a Thorough Move-Out Clean Should Include

Not all move-out cleans are created equal. If you're hiring a cleaning company — or even tackling it yourself — here's what a truly thorough move-out clean should cover:

### Kitchen

The kitchen is where landlords look the hardest. A good move-out clean should include:

- Inside and outside of the oven, stovetop, and range hood
- Interior of the refrigerator, freezer, and dishwasher
- All countertops, backsplashes, and cabinet fronts
- Sink scrubbed and faucet polished
- Inside cabinets and drawers wiped down (yes, even the empty ones)

### Bathrooms

Bathrooms are the second biggest area of scrutiny. Look for a clean that covers:

- Toilet cleaned inside and out, including the base
- Shower, tub, and tile scrubbed — grout included
- Mirrors, counters, and fixtures cleaned and streak-free
- Inside the medicine cabinet and under the sink
- Exhaust fan dusted

### Living Areas and Bedrooms

- Baseboards wiped down throughout
- Light switches, outlet covers, and door handles sanitized
- Ceiling fans and light fixtures dusted
- Closet shelves and rods wiped clean
- Floors vacuumed and mopped, including corners and edges
- Window sills and tracks cleaned out

### Often-Missed Spots

These are the areas that catch a lot of renters off guard during the final walkthrough:

- Blinds and window tracks (especially in Cache Valley where dust and pollen settle fast)
- Inside the laundry area — lint, dust, and residue around hookups
- Garage floors if applicable
- Front and back door thresholds
- Air vents and return covers

If you're comparing cleaning services in Logan, UT, make sure the company you hire covers these details. A surface-level wipe-down won't cut it when your deposit is on the line.

## When Should You Schedule Your Move-Out Clean?

Timing matters. The best time to schedule a move-out clean in Logan, UT is after all your belongings have been removed but before your final walkthrough. This gives your cleaning team full access to every surface, corner, and closet without furniture in the way.

If you're moving during a busy season — like the end of spring semester at USU or the start of summer — it's smart to book your clean at least a week or two in advance. Move-out cleans fill up fast here in Cache Valley, especially in May and August.
