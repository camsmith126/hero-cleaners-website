## What Commercial Restaurant Cleaning Looks Like

At Hero Cleaners, we understand that food service cleaning is different from office cleaning or residential work. Restaurants have unique needs — grease buildup, high-traffic floors, restrooms that see hundreds of visitors a week, and front-of-house areas that need to sparkle every single day.

Here's what we typically tackle for our Logan UT restaurant clients:

- **Dining area deep cleaning** — tables, chairs, booths, baseboards, light fixtures, and décor
- **Floor care** — mopping, scrubbing, and degreasing tile, concrete, and other commercial flooring
- **Window washing** — inside and out, so your storefront always looks inviting
- **Restroom sanitation** — thorough disinfecting, restocking checks, and fixture cleaning
- **Entryway and waiting area upkeep** — first impressions matter
- **Kitchen-adjacent cleaning** — while your kitchen crew handles food-contact surfaces, we can take care of walls, ceilings, vents, and non-cooking areas

Every restaurant is different, and we build our cleaning plans around your specific layout, schedule, and budget. There's no cookie-cutter approach here — just honest, flexible service from a team that genuinely cares about your business.
